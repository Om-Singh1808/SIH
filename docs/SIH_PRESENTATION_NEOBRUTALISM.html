Idea Title: RetailSense

Proposed Solution — Describe your Idea / Solution / Prototype
Detailed Explanation
  • RetailSense connects directly to a store's existing CCTV/DVR via standard IP stream — zero new camera hardware required.
  • A local edge AI engine (SenseEdge) performs real-time computer vision analysis, tracking anonymous store movement without face recognition.
  • Automatically calculates shelf inventory coverage, customer queue lengths, and store footfall entirely on-device with zero data loss during power/net cuts.
  • Sends actionable alerts in Hindi & English directly to WhatsApp with 1-tap quick response: 1 = भर दिया (Filled), 2 = ୟर्डर (Order), 3 = गलत (False).
How It Addresses the Problem
  • Shopper Analytics: Directional footfall counting, spatial store traffic heatmaps, and peak-hour customer flow analysis.
  • Inventory Monitoring: Automated shelf gap detection with multi-scan verification — immediate alerts before shelves go empty.
  • Queue Intelligence: Real-time checkout queue length and wait-time estimation to prevent customer queue abandonment.
  • Privacy-First Design: 100% on-device video processing. No face recognition, no identity tracking, and no video leaves the store.
  • POS & Catalog Sync: Visual shelf stock reconciliation with billing records and live digital catalog availability.
Innovation & Uniqueness (3 Key Differentiators)
Actionable WhatsApp Alerts

Direct Hindi/English messaging with 1-tap quick actions. Store owners receive instant out-of-stock and queue warnings without managing complex dashboards.

Offline-First Resilience

Engineered for Indian store conditions. Local edge storage logs all store events during internet or power outages and auto-syncs seamlessly upon reconnect.

Zero New Hardware Required

Works out-of-the-box with existing analog/IP CCTV cameras and low-cost micro-processors, eliminating high upfront equipment investments for small stores.

Feasibility and Viability

Feasibility analysis · Challenges & risks · Mitigation strategies · Roadmap
Feasibility Analysis
Technical Feasibility — PROVEN
  • Working end-to-end prototype running local AI detection and alert generation.
  • Lightweight vision models optimized for real-time execution on standard CPUs.
  • High-accuracy anonymous tracking without biometric or facial recognition.
  • Verified offline resilience with zero data loss during simulated power dropouts.
Operational & Deployment Viability — VIABLE
  • Zero camera hardware replacement — connects seamlessly to existing store DVRs.
  • Rapid 15-minute onboarding with zero disruption to daily shop operations.
  • Low power consumption (<8W) operating efficiently on local store power.
  • Intuitive vernacular interface requiring zero technical training for shop owners.
Challenges & Mitigation
HIGH
Cluttered multi-product shelves
Multi-scan temporal verification filters out transient visual occlusions to ensure high gap accuracy.
HIGH
Camera stream dropouts on older DVRs
Auto-reconnect stream handlers with offline frame buffering and camera health alerts.
MED
Messaging API delivery delays
Multi-channel notification dispatch (WhatsApp, Mobile Web Push, Telegram, SMS) for zero missed alerts.
LOW
Power or internet outages during store hours
Local-first edge architecture logs events locally and automatically synchronizes when connection returns.
